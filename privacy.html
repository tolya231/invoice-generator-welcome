Privacy Policy — Invoice Generator

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Invoice Generator (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension that turns a form into a numbered invoice and downloads it as a PDF file.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no backend, no account system, no analytics and no telemetry. We — the developer — never receive your business details, your clients, your amounts or any identifier. There is no server to receive them: the extension makes no network requests at all, which you can verify in Chrome’s DevTools Network tab.

What the extension stores

Everything you type stays in chrome.storage.local, inside your own browser profile on your own computer:

  • your business details: legal name, address, registration and tax number, bank account, IBAN, payment link;
  • your logo, if you upload one;
  • your clients and your saved line items;
  • the documents you issue, with their numbers, dates and amounts;
  • your settings: currency, locale, page size, layout, numbering and tax defaults.

None of it is uploaded anywhere. You can export all of it to a JSON file at any time from the settings page and import it back on another profile. Removing the extension removes its local storage along with it, so export first if you want to keep a copy.

Permissions

The extension requests one permission: storage, used solely to save the data described above. It asks for no host permissions and injects no content scripts, so it cannot read the pages you visit. It runs only in its own popup and its own pages, and only while you have them open.

The PDF file

The document is generated on your machine. The PDF library and the embedded font ship inside the extension package and are read from disk, not fetched from a CDN. The file is written straight to your downloads folder; it is not sent through any service, and no copy of it is kept anywhere but your own disk.

Third parties

The extension integrates with no external service and loads nothing from the network. It bundles one third-party library, jsPDF (MIT), and the Noto Sans font (SIL Open Font License); both are packaged locally, and their licences travel with them. No data is sold, shared or disclosed, because none is collected.

Children

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version starts handling data differently — for example an optional sync of your documents across your own devices — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the extension’s store listing.
