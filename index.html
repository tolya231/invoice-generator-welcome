Invoice Generator is installed

Two short steps and your first invoice is ready to send.

1 Pin it to the toolbar

Chrome hides new extensions behind the puzzle icon. Click it, find Invoice Generator in the list and click the pin, so a new invoice is always one click away.

2 Add your business details once

Open the extension and fill in your name, address, tax number and bank details. They go onto every document from then on, so the next invoice is only a client and a few lines. Everything stays in your browser — no account, no upload.

Need a quote, an estimate or a receipt instead? Same form, different heading.
